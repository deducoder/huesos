`Metrics` and `Scope verification` have no story or bug counterpart — not
ceremony, but what this skill's step 3 itself demands: an epic aggregates many
stories' estimates (hence `Stories: {n}`, not a single `Estimated: {x} ·
Actual: {y}` line), and "all stories done" is never trusted as "scope
fulfilled" — the scope is re-read item by item against the code, and that
re-read needs a place to land. A story's or bug's review confirms its handful
of criteria informally, in passing; it has no analogous aggregate to verify.
